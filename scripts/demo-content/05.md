A new account no longer opens on the full workspace. The person sees an empty window, and parts of the app appear one at a time in a fixed order: the sidebar, the helper, the list view, then the sample notes. The order never changes, and the person cannot skip it. The whole tour takes about 30 seconds and ends when the person saves their first note.

## The steps

1. **The empty window.** The person sees a blank window with no sidebar, no list and no notes. The text is at 125% zoom. The person can type anywhere. A blinking cursor is the only sign that typing works.
2. **The sidebar.** After 5 seconds, the sidebar slides in from the left edge. The app places the rest of the layout around the sidebar at that moment. The list and the notes keep today's layout, and the sidebar never moves after that. The person must open the sidebar's first item or press Ctrl-1. The dialog still says "Your lists".
3. **The helper.** Closing the dialog brings in the helper, a small card in the corner, with today's welcome line.
4. **The list view.** The helper points down at the list view. The window scrolls with it and keeps the sidebar in view, so the list slides into view even if the person does nothing. The person cannot scroll up in this step. The helper says "Everything you write ends up here".
5. **The sample notes.** The text zoom goes back to 100%, the default for a new account. Three sample notes fade into the list.
6. **The new note button.** The helper moves to the toolbar and lights up the New note button. The helper says today's line about new notes. The person moves the pointer to the button. Within about 40 pixels, the button grows a little and shows its shortcut. The settings icon appears, as it does today. Keyboard shortcuts turn on. The helper leaves.
7. **The first note.** The person types a note.
8. **The first save.** The tour ends here.

## The guide rule

One system runs every step. Each step names its goal: the sidebar, the New note button, the first note or the first save.

* The app hides any part of a step that leads away from the goal, so the person cannot get lost.
* Scrolling stops before the goal leaves the screen.
* After 6 seconds with no progress toward the goal, a line at the top of the window names the key, such as "\[Ctrl\]\[1\] open lists" or "\[Ctrl\]\[N\] new note". It uses the style of the settings page's title and hint line, with key caps. The line hides after the next correct action. The empty window shows "\[Ctrl\]\[1\] open lists" too if the person does nothing.
* At the first note step only, the helper comes back after 20 seconds with no typing. It says one friendly line once, about writing anything at all. The product owner writes that line.

## Other rules

* The New note button does nothing until the helper lights it up. The first note always starts from that button. The shortcut works after that.
* Enter or Space moves the helper's lines on faster.
* If the person quits before the first save, the next launch starts the tour again from the empty window.
* Accounts that already opened the sidebar skip the tour and need no data change.
* No import button shows in the window. [DEMO-104](https://example.com/demo/issues/DEMO-104) moves import into settings, so [DEMO-104](https://example.com/demo/issues/DEMO-104) must ship first.

## New systems

* **The helper moves to places.** The helper's script in `assets/helper.toml` gets a step that moves it to a named place, such as "list top" or "toolbar". It does not use raw coordinates. Later tours can use it too.
* **Hints on buttons.** A lit button grows a little and shows its shortcut. Within about 40 pixels, it also shows a short label, or a tooltip when the window is narrow. Later hints use the same system.
* **The guide.** It holds the step goals, the two scrolling rules and the top hint line.

## What changes in the app's docs

* `PRODUCT.md`, "The first launch": the full starting workspace, the empty toolbar and the sample notes all change.
* `PRODUCT.md`, "Import": the import button no longer shows on the first launch. See [DEMO-104](https://example.com/demo/issues/DEMO-104).
* `EVENTS.md`: the new script step that moves the helper to a place.
* A test script in `flows/` walks the whole tour.
